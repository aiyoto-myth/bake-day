A free production planner for micro bakeries. Put in your breads once and how many of each you're baking. Get the dough for every mix, one levain build for all of it, and a clock schedule worked back from when the last loaf leaves the oven. Print it for the wall by the oven.

1. Your breads and how many

Type each recipe as one batch in grams, or as baker's percentages. Only the ratios are used, so either works. Mark the line that is your levain.

2. Levain

One build for every bread that uses it. Give a levain line a different name in a recipe (say “Rye sour”) and it gets its own build here.

3. Bake day and your kit

Everything is worked back from the time the last loaf comes out.
